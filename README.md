One line, under 200 characters, about crypto and trading life — an entry for the
[make.fun](https://make.fun/bounty/u-alfasurya91-laugh-track-the-crypto-trading-joke-a454158a)
bounty "Laugh Track: The Crypto Trading Joke Contest".

`main.ts` writes the joke to `/output/entry.txt`. A house judge model reads the
entries two at a time and picks the funniest; the line that makes the judge laugh
hardest takes the top reward.

## The joke

> My trading strategy is simple: I buy the top, panic-sell the bottom, and call the group chat 'research' so it feels professional.
